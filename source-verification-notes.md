## Per-essay review checklist

Before treating an essay's proof spine as complete, verify that each central historical or experimental claim has a source that directly supports it; that paraphrases preserve what the source actually says and measures; that reported measurements are distinguished from independent replication; and that missing evidence is described as absent from the record checked, not as proof that an experiment never occurred. Confirm that every `\cite{...}` has a matching `\bibitem`, and that no bibliography entry is unused.

## Update convention

Append a dated note when a source is checked. Give enough identifying information to find it again, state what it supports, and record any discrepancy or limitation. Do not upgrade an item from unverified based only on a secondary claim repeated without a traceable source.
