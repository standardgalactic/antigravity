Verification means that the named bibliographic record and the cited source's direct support were checked. A publication record can verify that a work exists without verifying claims made about its experimental conclusions.

## Source-review standard

For each claim, cite only a source that directly supports it. A publisher or indexing record may confirm bibliographic metadata, but it does not prove a paper's experimental result. An inventor-authored source can establish what the inventor said or reported; it must not be described as independent replication. If no suitable primary source or published report is located, state the gap plainly, avoid converting search failure into proof of nonexistence, and do not retain unsupported numbers or dates.
