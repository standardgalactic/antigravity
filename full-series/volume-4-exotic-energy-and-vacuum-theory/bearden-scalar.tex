\begin{abstract}
Thomas Bearden has since the 1980s promoted a framework he calls scalar electromagnetics, proposing that a component of the electromagnetic potential ordinarily treated as a mathematical gauge freedom without independent physical effect can in fact be manipulated to produce free energy extraction, remote energy transmission, and directed-energy "scalar weapons," which Bearden has further claimed were secretly developed and deployed by the Soviet Union and, in some of his writing, connected to specific unexplained disasters and health events. This paper argues that gauge freedom in electromagnetism is not an unexplored or overlooked region of the theory but one of its most thoroughly examined structural features, with the physical electromagnetic fields, not the potentials themselves, established as the gauge-invariant quantities responsible for all observable electromagnetic effects, a result confirmed to extraordinary precision across the entire discipline, that Bearden's specific technical claims have been directly and repeatedly rebutted by physicists engaging his published equations on their own mathematical terms, and that no independently verified scalar weapon detonation, energy transmission event, or free energy device consistent with Bearden's framework has ever been documented outside his own extensive but self-published and self-referential body of writing.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Thomas Bearden, a retired U.S. Army officer with an engineering background, has published extensively since the 1980s on what he terms scalar electromagnetics, a framework proposing that the electromagnetic four-potential, a mathematical construct from which the physically measurable electric and magnetic fields are derived, possesses an independently manipulable component, sometimes described in terms of interfering or "phase conjugate" scalar potentials, capable of transmitting energy and information without the accompanying physical field ordinarily associated with electromagnetic radiation, a claim Bearden has extended into proposals for free energy extraction devices, long-range wireless power transmission, and directed-energy weapons he terms scalar weapons, which he has further claimed were developed by the Soviet Union and used to cause specific historical events including, in various of his writings, particular earthquakes, weather events, and the 1986 Chernobyl disaster.

\section{Why Gauge Freedom Is a Well Understood Structural Feature, Not an Overlooked Loophole}

In classical electromagnetism, the electric and magnetic fields can be derived from a scalar and vector potential, but the potentials themselves are not uniquely determined by the fields, a mathematical property called gauge freedom, meaning different choices of potential, related by a specific class of transformation, produce identical physical electric and magnetic fields and therefore identical predictions for every measurable electromagnetic effect, a result established since the nineteenth century development of the theory and confirmed as a foundational and thoroughly examined structural feature of both classical electrodynamics and its quantum extension, quantum electrodynamics, one of the most precisely experimentally confirmed theories in physics. This gauge freedom is not an unexplored region where an alternative, physically effective potential configuration might be hiding undetected; it is a mathematical redundancy in how the theory is formulated, with the gauge-invariant fields themselves, not any particular choice of potential, established as the physically real quantities responsible for every observed electromagnetic interaction, a conclusion that follows directly from the mathematics rather than from an incomplete historical exploration of the theory's structure.

\section{What Direct Technical Rebuttal Has Found}

Bearden's specific published derivations proposing physically effective, independently measurable scalar potential interactions have received direct technical engagement from physicists working through his equations on their own terms, identifying specific mathematical errors, including misapplication of the relevant field equations and conflation of mathematical gauge transformations with physically distinct field configurations, rather than dismissing the work without engagement, a pattern comparable to the direct technical rebuttal Marinov's relativity claims received, addressed elsewhere in this series, and one that Bearden's extensive subsequent published responses have not been shown, by independent physicists reviewing the exchange, to have adequately resolved on the specific mathematical points raised.

\section{The Absence of Any Independently Documented Device or Event}

Despite several decades of extensive publication describing scalar weapon capabilities and specific historical events attributed to their use, no independently verified scalar weapon test, detonation, or transmission event consistent with Bearden's framework has been documented by any historian, seismologist, or nuclear safety investigator working on the specific events Bearden has attributed to scalar weapon use, including the Chernobyl disaster, whose causes have been extensively and independently investigated through conventional nuclear engineering analysis with no unexplained anomaly requiring a scalar weapon explanation, and no free energy or wireless power transmission device built according to Bearden's published specifications has been independently demonstrated to function as claimed under third-party testing.

\section{The Entropic Gravity Framing}

Bearden's scalar electromagnetics primarily concerns electromagnetic theory rather than gravitation directly, though some of his writing extends the framework toward gravitational and antigravity applications using the same scalar potential vocabulary, and is included in this series as a comparative case in the "overlooked mathematical structure as hidden physical mechanism" genre alongside the torsion field and Kozyrev causal mechanics entries, illustrating a recurring pattern of treating a well understood, thoroughly examined mathematical feature of an established theory as an unexplored physical frontier without independent confirmation that the feature carries the additional physical content being proposed.

\section{Objections}

Supporters argue that Bearden's military and engineering background lends his technical claims credibility that a purely civilian or academic critic's dismissal fails to adequately weigh, and that mainstream physics has a demonstrated history of initially dismissing eventually vindicated ideas. Professional background in engineering or military service does not substitute for the specific mathematical rebuttal his published derivations have received on their own technical terms, addressed above, and the general historical observation that some dismissed ideas are eventually vindicated does not, on its own, provide evidence for any specific currently unvindicated idea, a distinction this objection, common across several claims in this series, consistently elides.

\section{Conclusion}

Gauge freedom in electromagnetic theory is a thoroughly examined and well understood mathematical structure in which only the gauge-invariant fields, not the potentials themselves, carry independently measurable physical effect, Bearden's specific claims to the contrary have received direct technical rebuttal engaging his own published mathematics, and no independently verified scalar weapon event or functioning free energy device consistent with his framework has been documented outside his own extensive self-published body of work.
